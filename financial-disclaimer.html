Assumes:

Retirement Projections

Assumes:

4. Market Risk Disclaimer

All investments carry risk, including:

  • Market Risk: Asset values fluctuate with market conditions
  • Interest Rate Risk: Bond values decrease when rates rise
  • Credit Risk: Issuers may default on obligations
  • Liquidity Risk: Some assets cannot be quickly sold
  • Inflation Risk: Purchasing power may decline
  • Concentration Risk: Overweighting one asset class or security

Past performance is not indicative of future results. The S&P 500 may return 7% on average, but this includes years with -30% to +40% returns.
